Common AC Problems

  • AC blows warm air - Low refrigerant, compressor failure, or electrical issues
  • Weak airflow - Clogged cabin filter or blower motor problems
  • Intermittent cooling - Moisture in system or failing clutch
  • Strange smells - Mold, coolant leak, or electrical problems
  • AC only works on high - Blower motor resistor failure
  • Unusual noises - Compressor bearing or debris in blower

Understanding Your AC System

Your vehicle's AC system is complex, with several components working together to cool your cabin:

Compressor

Pumps refrigerant through the system. The heart of your AC system.

Condenser

Cools hot refrigerant. Located at front of vehicle, vulnerable to road debris.

Evaporator

Creates cold air inside cabin. Hidden behind dashboard.

Expansion Valve

Regulates refrigerant flow and pressure.

Preventive AC Maintenance

Keep Your AC Running Strong

Run AC Year-Round:

Run your AC for 10 minutes weekly, even in winter. This keeps seals lubricated and prevents compressor seizing.

Replace Cabin Filter Regularly:

Every 12,000-15,000 miles (more often in Tulsa due to high pollen). Clogged filters dramatically reduce airflow.

Annual AC Check (Pre-Summer):

  • Performance test
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Visual leak inspection
  • Belt and hose inspection

Keep Condenser Clean:

Remove bugs and debris from condenser. Check after highway trips. Bent fins reduce efficiency.
