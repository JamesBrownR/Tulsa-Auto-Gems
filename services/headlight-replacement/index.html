Signs You Need Headlight Service

  • One or both headlights out — Burned-out bulb or wiring issue
  • Yellow or foggy lenses — UV oxidation reducing output by up to 80%
  • Flickering headlights — Failing bulb or loose connection
  • Dim light output — Aging bulbs or clouded lenses
  • Headlight warning on dash — Bulb failure detected by your vehicle's computer
  • Failed inspection — Oklahoma requires functioning headlights

Restoration vs. Replacement — Which Do You Need?

A lot of drivers assume they need brand-new headlight assemblies when their lights look dim or foggy. In many cases, professional restoration is all that's needed — and it saves significant money. Here's a simple guide:

Choose Restoration If...

  • Lenses are yellow or hazy
  • Housing has no cracks or damage
  • No moisture inside the lens
  • You want to save money

Choose Replacement If...

  • Housing is cracked or broken
  • Moisture or condensation inside
  • Previous restoration hasn't held
  • Internal reflector is damaged

Not sure which you need? Bring your vehicle in and we'll take a look for free and give you an honest recommendation.

Headlight Service FAQ

How long does headlight restoration take?

Most restoration jobs take 1–2 hours. We can often do it while you wait or while another service is being performed.

How long does restoration last?

With our professional UV sealant application, results typically last 2–4 years. DIY kits from auto parts stores often fade within months because they skip the UV protection step.

Is it illegal to drive with a burned-out headlight in Oklahoma?

Yes. Oklahoma law requires functioning headlights from dusk to dawn and during reduced visibility. Driving with a burned-out headlight can result in a ticket and puts you and others at risk.

Can I just replace one headlight bulb?

You can, but we usually recommend replacing both at the same time. If one bulb has burned out, the other is often close behind. Matching bulbs also ensures even light output and appearance.

Do you offer a warranty on headlight work?

Yes. All parts and labor are covered by our standard warranty. We'll go over the specifics when you bring your vehicle in.
