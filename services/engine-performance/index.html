Oil Change Intervals

Recommended Service Intervals

Conventional Oil:

Every 3,000-5,000 miles or 3-6 months. Ideal for older vehicles or those with higher mileage.

Synthetic Oil:

Every 5,000-7,500 miles or 6-12 months. Better protection for modern engines, especially in extreme temperatures.

Oklahoma Considerations:

  • Hot summer temperatures break down oil faster
  • Stop-and-go Tulsa traffic is "severe service"
  • Short trips (under 10 miles) require more frequent changes
  • Follow the lower end of the mileage range for best protection

Check Engine Light Diagnosis

Don't ignore your check engine light! While it might not be an emergency, it indicates a problem that needs attention.

Common Causes

  • Loose or damaged gas cap
  • Oxygen sensor failure
  • Catalytic converter issues
  • Mass airflow sensor problems
  • Spark plug or ignition coil failure
  • EVAP system leaks

Our Diagnostic Process

  • Computer code scanning
  • Live data analysis
  • Visual inspection
  • Component testing
  • Root cause identification
  • Repair recommendation

⚠️ Flashing Check Engine Light = Emergency

A flashing check engine light indicates a serious problem (usually a misfire) that can damage your catalytic converter. Pull over safely and have your vehicle towed to our shop immediately.
