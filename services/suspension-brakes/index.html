Safe Stopping & Smooth Handling

Your Safety Depends on Your Brakes

Your brakes are your vehicle's most important safety system. In Tulsa's stop-and-go traffic on I-44, Highway 169, and the BA Expressway, your brakes work hard every day. Regular maintenance ensures optimal performance when you need it most.

Warning Signs You Need Brake Service

  • Squealing or grinding noises when braking
  • Soft or spongy brake pedal
  • Vibration or pulsing when braking
  • Vehicle pulls to one side when braking
  • Brake warning light on dashboard

Don't ignore these signs! Early attention prevents expensive repairs and keeps you safe.

Signs You Need Suspension Service

  • Clunking or rattling noises over bumps
  • Excessive bouncing after hitting bumps
  • Vehicle pulls to one side
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Nose dives when braking
  • Difficulty steering or loose steering feel
