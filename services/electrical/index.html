Common Electrical Problems

  • Won't start or slow cranking - Dead battery, failing starter, or poor connections

Battery Replacement Guide

When to Replace Your Battery

By Age:

  • 3-5 years is typical battery life in Oklahoma
  • Extreme heat shortens battery life significantly
  • Replace proactively at 4 years to avoid being stranded

Warning Signs:

  • Slow engine cranking, especially when cold
  • Dim headlights at idle
  • Clicking sound when trying to start
  • Battery warning light on dashboard
  • Swollen or bloated battery case
  • Corrosion on terminals

Oklahoma Considerations:

  • Summer heat is harder on batteries than winter cold
  • Temperature extremes accelerate failure
  • Get battery tested every fall before winter
  • Don't wait for battery to fail completely

Understanding Your Charging System

Three main components keep your electrical system running:

Battery

Stores electrical energy and provides power to start the engine. Supplies power when alternator can't meet demand or when engine is off. Typical life: 3-5 years.

Alternator

Generates electricity while engine runs. Recharges battery and powers electrical systems. Contains voltage regulator to maintain proper charging. Signs of failure: dimming lights, battery light on, electrical issues.

Starter

Electric motor that cranks the engine to start it. Draws heavy current from battery. Solenoid engages starter drive to flywheel. Signs of failure: clicking sound, slow cranking, no response when key turned.

Common Electrical Issues by Symptom

Car won't start (clicking sound):

Likely causes: Weak battery, poor battery connections, failing starter solenoid

Car won't start (no sound):

Likely causes: Dead battery, failed starter, ignition switch problem, security system issue

Battery keeps dying:

Likely causes: Failing alternator, parasitic drain, old battery, corroded connections

Lights dim when idling:

Likely causes: Weak alternator, loose or corroded connections, excessive electrical load

Battery warning light on:

Likely causes: Alternator failure, loose or broken belt, poor connections, voltage regulator failure
