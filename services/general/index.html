Manufacturer Maintenance Schedules

We follow manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedules to keep your vehicle warranty valid and ensure optimal performance. Here's what we recommend:

Every 5,000-7,500 Miles

  • Oil and filter change
  • Tire rotation
  • Multi-point inspection
  • Fluid level checks
  • Brake inspection

Every 15,000 Miles

  • Replace engine air filter
  • Replace cabin air filter
  • Inspect belts and hoses
  • Check battery condition
  • Inspect suspension components

Every 30,000 Miles

  • Transmission fluid service
  • Coolant system flush
  • Fuel filter replacement
  • Replace PCV valve
  • Inspect drive belts

Every 60,000 Miles

  • Replace spark plugs
  • Timing belt inspection/replacement (if due)
  • Differential fluid service
  • Replace serpentine belt
  • Brake fluid exchange

Every 100,000 Miles

  • Major milestone service
  • Replace all coolant hoses
  • Inspect valve cover gaskets
  • Check engine/transmission mounts
  • Comprehensive brake system service

⚠️ Note: These are general guidelines. Always consult your owner's manual for your specific vehicle's maintenance requirements. Some models may have different intervals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I service my vehicle?

Follow your vehicle's maintenance schedule. Typically, oil changes every 5,000-7,500 miles, tire rotations every 5,000-7,500 miles, and major services at 30,000, 60,000, 90,000, and 100,000 mile intervals.

Can I use synthetic oil in my older vehicle?

Yes! Synthetic oil is suitable for most older vehicles and can actually provide better protection, especially in extreme temperatures. We can recommend the best oil for your specific vehicle.

What's included in your multi-point inspection?

We inspect brakes, tires, fluids, belts, hoses, battery, lights, suspension, exhaust, and more. You'll receive a detailed report of our findings with recommendations for any needed services.

Do you use OEM parts?

We primarily use OEM-quality or better parts to ensure reliability and longevity. For some services, we can offer aftermarket options if you prefer, and we'll explain the differences.

How long does typical maintenance take?

Oil changes take 30-45 minutes. Most routine services can be completed in 1-2 hours. We'll provide an accurate time estimate when you schedule your appointment.
